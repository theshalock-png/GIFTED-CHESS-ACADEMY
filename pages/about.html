Welcome to Gifted Chess Academy

Founded in 2015, Gifted Chess Academy has grown from a small community chess club into one of the region's premier chess training centers. Our mission is simple: to provide world-class chess education in a supportive, inclusive environment where players of all ages and skill levels can thrive.

Our philosophy centers on holistic development — we don't just teach chess moves; we cultivate critical thinking, strategic planning, patience, and sportsmanship. Our students don't just become better chess players; they become better thinkers and competitors in all aspects of life.

With a team of 15+ certified FIDE trainers, national-level players, and passionate coaches, we offer comprehensive programs ranging from beginner classes for young children to advanced tournament preparation for competitive players. Whether you dream of becoming a grandmaster or simply want to enjoy the game, Gifted Chess Academy is your home.
