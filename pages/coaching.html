Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you cater to?
We welcome players from age 5 to adults of all ages. Our programs are specifically designed for different age groups and skill levels to ensure optimal learning.
Do I need any prior chess knowledge to join?
No! Our Junior Beginners program is perfect for complete beginners. We'll teach you everything from how the pieces move to basic strategy.
How do I pay for membership?
We accept bank transfer, card payments, and cash. Monthly memberships are billed at the start of each month. First session is free for new members!
Can I try a session before committing?
Absolutely! We offer a free trial session for all new members. Come experience our academy, meet our coaches, and see if it's the right fit for you.
Do you organize tournaments?
Yes! We run monthly internal tournaments for all skill levels, and our competitive team represents the academy at regional and national tournaments throughout the year.
